## Interpretation guardrails

- The Stage-9 method is **not a fixed-threshold catalogue**: generated bounds depend on the particular profiling run, use median/MAD with `mad_z_limit=3.5`, fall back to quantiles `0.01/0.99` when MAD is zero, and need at least 20 finite reference values (`validation_rule_generation.py:62-72,297-358`).
- Stage-10 applies generated bounds for `sample_count`, `finite_sample_count`, `coverage_ratio`, `maximum_timestamp_gap`, and `signal_range`; it does **not** apply generated sampling-rate, interval, or standard-deviation rules (`dataset_validation.py:129-161`).
- This catalogue intentionally preserves the decision tree's proposed physical limits as proposed/absent rather than treating the pipeline's learned statistical limits as equivalent.
